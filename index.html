A Minimalist Fall Skincare Routine: Fewer Products, Better Skin

When the leaves turn amber and the air gets cooler, your skin notices before you do. Cooler, drier air pulls moisture from your skin faster than summer air does, so the glow you had in summer can turn dull, tight, or flaky. The best autumn routine is usually the simplest one: cleanse gently, exfoliate only once or twice a week, and finish with a richer moisturizer to lock in hydration.

Quick Tips

  • Patch test new products first
  • Wear sunscreen every morning
  • Exfoliate only once or twice a week
  • Use lukewarm water, not hot
